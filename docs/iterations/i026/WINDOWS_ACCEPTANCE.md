# I026 Windows acceptance: a morning, a day and an evening

Play it, don't tick boxes in a hurry. Use the Windows build of `release/i026`
(build label `0.112a`, channel TEST), at 1280×720 or larger. Do each part once
in English and once in Danish (Settings → Language). Linux tests passed. These
checks are the Captain's.

Start a new game in Copenhagen with a companion. Then load an **old save**
(made with I025 or earlier) and check it opens aboard, with money, boats,
companions and journals intact.

## A morning in harbour

1. Wake aboard in Copenhagen (sleep in the berth until about 07:00).
2. On deck, walk down to the pontoon. One sign says **To the quay**. Walk up the walkway.
3. On the quay:
   - [ ] Every business has its own building: boatyard (with the travel lift), sail loft, chandlery, harbour office with the noticeboard, café, grocery, fishmonger's stall, the lane into town.
   - [ ] Walking past a door makes it glow and shows a prompt with its state ("open until …" or "closed · opens …").
4. At 07:00 the café and fishmonger are open, while the chandlery and sail loft are still shut.
   - [ ] E at the chandlery offers **Wait until it opens**. Waiting moves the clock to its opening and says it is open now.
5. Café:
   - [ ] Order a coffee (EUR 4). The Captain sits at a terrace table with a mug.
   - [ ] Your companion settles nearby. Other customers come and go.
   - [ ] E or a movement key stands you up.
6. Noticeboard:
   - [ ] It shows a few cozy notes, no quests.
7. Harbour office:
   - [ ] The hours of every business are listed.
8. Shops:
   - [ ] Chandlery: buying a small item works as before.
   - [ ] Chandlery **Electronics**: buy and fit **Modern Cruiser**.
   - [ ] Grocery: fill food and water. Prices are as before.
   - [ ] Fishmonger: sell fish, if you have any.
9. People:
   - [ ] A few people walk, pause, go into shops and come out.
   - [ ] They step round the Captain and never block a door.
10. Walk back down the walkway.
    - [ ] You are on the pontoon by the walkway.
11. Save on the quay (F5).
    - [ ] Load that save: you are aboard by the walkway.

## A day at sea

1. Plan a course at the chart table and set sail.
2. Instruments:
   - [ ] The HUD shows the Modern Cruiser displays.
   - [ ] Press **I**: the focused instrument view opens. Page keys change pages, and the choice is remembered after reload.
   - [ ] Switch back to **Classic** in the view's selector. It fits instantly, at no cost.
   - [ ] At night the displays go red or dim.
3. Plotter:
   - [ ] Walk to the helm. E at the plotter (or **P**) opens the chartplotter, with range keys. Esc closes it.
4. Winch:
   - [ ] E at the **winch** on the cockpit coaming opens the sail trim, as T does.
   - [ ] Moored, the winch says why not.
5. Warnings:
   - [ ] Weather and collision warnings still appear in the HUD without opening anything.
   - [ ] The radar still pops up on a risk.
6. VHF:
   - [ ] Go below when a VHF call comes. The VHF set's lamp blinks.
   - [ ] E on it shows the call; **Answer the call** opens the usual dialog. The weather broadcast lines make sense.
7. Quick actions:
   - [ ] The action wheel (Q) still offers what you need underway.
   - [ ] Nothing you used before is missing.

## An evening aboard

1. Moored in the evening:
   - [ ] On the quay the shops close one by one, and a shopkeeper locks up.
   - [ ] Windows are lit.
   - [ ] The hatch at the harbour office still sells food and water.
   - [ ] With a damaged hull, the hatch offers an emergency repair.
2. In the cabin, use each thing with E. Each opens framed as the object, and **Esc** closes it:
   - [ ] the **logbook** (the Captain's Log, as a book)
   - [ ] the **VHF set**
   - [ ] the **switch panel** (hull and care survey, gear, sails, food and water, instruments; no battery or fuel)
   - [ ] the **sea chest** ("Make it home")
   - [ ] the stove and the provisions locker
   - [ ] the trophy board
   - [ ] the berth's alarm
3. Postcard:
   - [ ] Make a postcard on the laptop. It appears on the saloon table, and E opens the scrapbook at it.
4. Companions:
   - [ ] Your companions keep their places and never block an object.
5. Display and input:
   - [ ] At 1024×768 and at UI scale 125 %, every focused view and the quay's prompts fit, in English and Danish.
   - [ ] Mouse and keyboard work.
6. Performance:
   - [ ] Sitting on the quay and sailing at 20× feel as smooth as I025.

Report anything odd with the build label, the place and the time of day.
